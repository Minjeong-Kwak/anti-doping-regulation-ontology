# National decisions included as examples

These decisions are examples. They are not a corpus, they are not a sample of one, and no statement about how often anything occurs may be drawn from them. Each was selected because it exercises a structure the appeal register does not exercise.

## Decisions considered and not included

Four first instance awards of the American Arbitration Association whose appeals are in the register were considered and left out: the documents as published carry no text layer, and transcribing them by optical recognition would put reading errors into the source data. They are the awards concerning Hamilton, Thompson, Trafeh and Gil Roberts.

## Fields omitted because the decision does not state them

- AAA No. 30 190 00170 07: The award as published states no date of issue, so none is asserted.
- AAA No. 30 190 00170 07: no provision is asserted, because the award as published states no provision number for the violation found.
